Medical records are produced by care processes, not by controlled experiments. This sample theme develops analysis methods that retain that context.

The work spans temporal cohorts, data-quality assessment, interpretable summaries, and reproducible pipelines for collaborative studies.

## Current questions

- How can missingness be treated as part of the care process rather than noise?
- Which cohort definitions remain stable across institutions?
- How should analytical provenance be reported to clinical collaborators?
