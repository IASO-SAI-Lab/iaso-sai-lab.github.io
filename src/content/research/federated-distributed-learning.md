Healthcare organisations often need to learn together while operating under different technical, legal, and clinical constraints.

This sample area studies algorithms and system architectures for collaboration under non-identical data, partial availability, and strict governance.

## Current questions

- What happens when participating sites join, leave, or change over time?
- How should contribution and uncertainty be reported?
- Which guarantees remain meaningful under real operational constraints?
