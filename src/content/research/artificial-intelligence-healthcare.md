This sample research area studies how machine-learning systems can assist health professionals without obscuring uncertainty, provenance, or limitations.

Work in this area connects representation learning with clinical workflow analysis. The emphasis is not only on predictive performance, but on whether a system can support a defensible decision in context.

## Current questions

- How should models communicate uncertainty to different clinical roles?
- Which evaluation settings reveal brittle behaviour before deployment?
- How can multimodal records be aligned without flattening their clinical meaning?
