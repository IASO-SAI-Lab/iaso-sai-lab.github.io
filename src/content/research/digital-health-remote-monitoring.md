Care increasingly extends past the ward. This sample topic examines how signals gathered at home — from wearables, sensors, and patient-reported measures — become evidence a clinician can act on.

The difficulty is rarely the sensor. It is intermittent adherence, uneven data quality, and the question of who reviews an alert at three in the morning.

## Current questions

- Which remote signals change a decision, and which only add volume?
- How should a monitoring system behave when a stream degrades or stops?
- What escalation path keeps a continuous alert within an ordinary clinical workload?
